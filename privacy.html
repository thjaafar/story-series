StorySeries — Privacy Policy Last updated: October 14, 2026 1. What StorySeries Is StorySeries is an automated tool that creates story videos and publishes them to the TikTok account of a user who has explicitly authorized it, using the official TikTok Content Posting API. 2. Information We Collect TikTok authorization tokens. When you connect your TikTok account, TikTok issues OAuth tokens (access and refresh tokens) which we store so the Service can publish videos on your behalf. Access tokens expire and are refreshed automatically. Video content. Scripts and finished videos generated for your account are stored so they can be published on your schedule. Posting configuration. Your chosen posting times, series settings, and preferences. 3. What We Do NOT Collect We do not collect your TikTok password — authorization happens entirely on TikTok's website. We do not access your TikTok direct messages, contacts, or content unrelated to publishing. We do not sell or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. 4. How Information Is Used Collected information is used only to: (a) publish videos to your TikTok account as you instructed, (b) refresh your authorization so publishing continues uninterrupted, and (c) keep a record of what was published and when. 5. Data Sharing We share data only with the services required to operate: TikTok (for publishing, under their Privacy Policy), our AI provider (for script generation — scripts are processed without personal identifiers), and royalty-free media providers (for stock footage and music). 6. Data Retention & Deletion Your tokens and generated content are retained while your account is connected. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), after which we delete stored tokens and your generated content within 30 days. To request immediate deletion, contact us below. 7. Security Tokens are stored encrypted at rest and transmitted only over HTTPS. Access to production systems is restricted to the operator of the Service. 8. Children's Privacy The Service is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. 9. Changes to This Policy We may update this Policy; the "Last updated" date above reflects the current version.
